Privacy

Last updated 10.10.2026 · Norsk · Polski

In short

Hefau has no accounts, no ads and no profiling, and we sell no data. The game sets no cookies.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Jacek Mariusz Taczała (a private individual, the author of the game), contact: personvern@stop60.no.

Cookie-free statistics

We count visits with GoatCounter (the script is hosted with the game): page and referring page, browser and system, language, screen width, country (derived from the IP address) and the events “game started” and “game finished”. The IP address and browser are only used in the server's memory (up to 8 hours) to count unique visits; only aggregate statistics are stored, without IP address or identifier.

Purpose: to know how many people play and where they come from. Legal basis: legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); you can object, e.g. by blocking scripts.

Data on your device

The browser's storage (localStorage) keeps your language, sound setting, a game in progress (so you can continue) and, when you play online, the room code and a random technical ID for your seat. These are not cookies and do not leave the device, except what online play needs (below). You can delete them in your browser's settings for this site.

Online play

When you play online, the moves, game state, room code and the random technical ID are sent via Supabase Realtime to the other phone. The messages are only broadcast between the players in the room and are not stored by us. No names, accounts or contact details are used.

Recipients

Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd., Singapore, part of Supabase, Inc., USA) – the real-time connection for online play, on servers in the EU (AWS, Ireland), as processor under a data processing agreement (DPA) with EU standard contractual clauses for any access from outside the EEA; server logs may briefly record the IP address (Supabase privacy policy).

GoatCounter (Martin Tournoij, Ireland) – statistics, servers at Hetzner in Finland and Germany.

GitHub, Inc. (USA) – hosting via GitHub Pages; GitHub may log the IP address and browser data when pages are loaded.

E-mails to stop60.no addresses are forwarded by Domeneshop AS (Norway) to Proton AG (Switzerland) and only used to reply.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and to object. Write to personvern@stop60.no. You can also complain to Datatilsynet (Norway) or UODO (Poland).
